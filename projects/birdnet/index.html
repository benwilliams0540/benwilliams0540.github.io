BirdNET Edge Monitor

A local-first bird-monitoring node that runs BirdNET inference at the edge.

A dedicated field listener built on a Raspberry Pi 5 that identifies birds on-device with BirdNET, stores detections locally, and shares compact findings across low-bandwidth mesh networks — designed for conservation monitoring beyond reliable connectivity.

RoleHardware prototype, Swift rewrite, and mesh-sync architecture
